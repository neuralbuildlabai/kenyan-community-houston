# Vendor signup — vendor-facing copy

Plain-language copy meant to be read by **vendors**, not
organizers. Use it as:

- The contents of an event's `vendor_signup_instructions`
  field (max 1000 characters — the longer blocks below are
  trimmed versions for that purpose).
- A standalone public page in a future iteration (e.g.
  `/events/vendor-info`).
- A WhatsApp / email / flyer blurb when promoting vendor
  spots.

All copy is written in the project's editorial tone (warm,
clear, no marketing fluff).

## C. Full vendor-facing page copy (for a future
`/vendors` or per-event landing page)

### Sell at a KIGH community event

Bringing your business to a KIGH event is one of the best
ways to reach the Kenyan community in Greater Houston —
families, professionals, newcomers, churches, and friends of
Kenya all show up. We keep the application simple and the
fees affordable so small businesses, side hustles, and
nonprofits can all participate.

### How it works

1. **Find an event.** Browse the
   [community calendar](/events) and pick an event with a
   "Sign up as a vendor" button. Not every event has vendor
   spots open — those that do will show the button on the
   right side of the event page.
2. **Fill out the short form.** We ask for your business
   name, the person we should contact, an email, a phone
   number, your vendor category (food or other), and a short
   description of what you'll be selling. The form takes
   about two minutes.
3. **Send your fee.** As soon as you submit, you'll see three
   payment options — CashApp, Venmo, and PayPal — for the
   KIGH treasurer. Send the fee using whichever is easiest
   for you. **Include your business name in the payment
   note** so we can match it to your signup.
4. **We confirm your spot.** A KIGH organizer marks your
   signup as paid once the funds arrive, and you'll hear from
   us with setup details a few days before the event.

### Fees

- **Food vendor — $100**
- **All other vendors (retail, apparel, services, nonprofit,
  community group) — $50**

Fees go toward venue logistics, permits, signage, and the
volunteer labor that makes the event run. The treasurer
handles all payments; KIGH does not collect payments through
the website.

### Payment handles

When you submit the form, the thank-you screen will show all
three of these so you can tap one and pay directly. They're
listed here as well in case you want to send the fee before
heading to the form:

- **CashApp** — `$KighTreasurer`
- **Venmo** — `@KIGH_Treasurer`
- **PayPal** — `@KighTreasurer`

If you choose to pay before submitting the form, still
complete the form afterward so we have your contact info on
file.

### What to expect at the event

Setup time, table availability, electrical access, tent
rules, and food-permit requirements vary by event. Anything
event-specific will be listed in the **Instructions** section
of that event's vendor signup page, and we'll email you
final details a few days before the event date.

### Questions?

- Pricing or fee questions → KIGH treasurer (handles above).
- Setup, logistics, vendor mix → the event organizer (listed
  on the event page).
- Site or signup issues → use the
  [Contact](/contact) page or reach a KIGH community admin.

### A note on privacy

Your **business name** and **product/service description**
may appear on a public vendor list for the event. Your
**contact name, email, and phone number** are only visible to
KIGH organizers — they are not published anywhere on the
site.
